Biosketch

Abu Yousuf Md Abdullah is a research professional with an MSc in Public Health and Health Systems degree from the University of Waterloo, Canada. Abdullah has been working in the public health planning sector for nearly eight years, with a three-year-long experience of working as a Senior Research Officer in the Infectious Diseases Division (Program on Emerging Infections) at the International Centre for Diarrhoeal Disease Research, Bangladesh (icddr,b) and one and half year experience of working as a Research Associate in the School of Planning and School of Public Health Sciences at the University of Waterloo, Canada. Additionally, Abdullah has worked as a Researcher in the Data, Surveillance, and Foresight Branch of the Public Health Agency of Canada (Government of Canada).

Abdullah has worked on several national and international projects, which include (but are not limited to) the NSERC Discovery Grant Project, CFI Innovation Fund Project, Child Health and Mortality Prevention Surveillance (CHAMPS), USAID MaMoni Maternal and Newborn Strengthening and the Interrupting Pathways to Sepsis Initiative (IPSI) Projects. Notable achievements of Abdullah include (but are not limited to) receiving the Ontario Government Scholarship, President's Scholarship, School of Planning Award of Merit, the UWaterloo's Faculty of Environment Entrance Scholarship for being an Exceptional Student, and the Dean's Doctoral Initiative, the Faculty of Applied Health Sciences Award , the University of Dhaka's Undergraduate Scholarship for Academic Excellence, and the UCLA-Harvard's 6th International Climate Geoengineering Summer School Scholarship.

Abdullah has published 28 peer-reviewed articles (eight lead-authored) and is a reviewer of prominent international journals such as Nature Communications, Nature Scientific Reports, JAMA Network Open, PLoS One, PLoS Neglected Tropical Diseases, Remote Sensing, BMJ Paediatrics Open, BMC Tropical Medicine and Health, Applied Sciences, Earth Systems and Environment and others. Abu Yousuf Md Abdullah is currently a PhD Candidate at the School of Planning, University of Waterloo, Canada. Abdullah's doctoral research focuses on AI applications in Planning and developing Bayesian deep learning algorithms for analyzing high-dimensional rich/refined big geospatial data. Abdullah is now working as a 'Doctoral Researcher' in the Canada Synthetic Health Data Network for Health AI Innovation under NRC-Waterloo Collaboration on Artificial Intelligence, Internet of Things, and Cybersecurity – Phase III
