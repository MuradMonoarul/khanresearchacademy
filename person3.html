Biosketch

Dr. Imrul Kayes is an environmental biogeochemist with research experience spanning forest and urban ecosystems. Dr. Kayes completed his PhD in Forestry, with a Collaborative Specialization in Environmental Studies, at the University of Toronto, Canada. He also holds an MSc in Biology from Lakehead University, Canada, where his research focused on post-fire black spruce regeneration in boreal forests, as well as MSc and BSc degrees in Forestry from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh. Prior to his doctoral studies, Dr. Kayes served as a Lecturer and Assistant Professor in the Department of Environmental Science and Disaster Management at Noakhali Science and Technology University, Bangladesh, where he taught undergraduate and graduate courses and supervised student research.

Dr. Kayes's research focuses on environmental biogeochemistry, particularly how natural and anthropogenic disturbances and management interventions regulate greenhouse-gas exchange, soil processes, and ecosystem recovery. His doctoral research investigated methane dynamics across heterogeneous urban ecosystems and evaluated biochar as a nature-based strategy for greenhouse-gas mitigation. His research integrates long-term field experimentation, chamber-based measurements of CH₄, CO₂, and N₂O, soil biogeochemistry, mobile atmospheric surveys, geospatial and remote-sensing analyses, and data-driven modelling. His work has examined urban forests, wetlands, green roofs, street-tree systems, and other managed urban soils, while his earlier research addressed wildfire-driven regeneration in Canadian boreal forests.

Dr. Kayes has published peer-reviewed research in journals including Environmental Science & Technology, Journal of Environmental Management, Carbon Research, Forest Ecology and Management, and Air Quality, Atmosphere & Health, and has contributed to book chapters published by Springer Nature. He has presented his research at national and international scientific conferences, including the European Geosciences Union, Ecological Society of America–Canadian Society of Ecology and Evolution, and Canadian Society for Ecology and Evolution meetings. He also contributes to the scientific community as a reviewer for international journals including Air Quality, Atmosphere & Health, Urban Climate, Environment, Development and Sustainability, Carbon Management, and Big Earth Data. His current research interests center on environmental biogeochemistry across urban and forest ecosystems, with particular emphasis on greenhouse-gas cycling, biochar and pyrogenic carbon, forest disturbance, and climate-responsive ecosystem management.
