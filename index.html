About the Academy

DR. KHAN RESEARCH ACADEMY

As a forward-thinking sister concern of the Dr. Khan Social Impact Lab, Dr. Khan Research Academy is redefining academic enablement by bridging the gap between raw curiosity and high-impact scholarship. Through our core framework—LEARN, PRACTICE, CREATE, and GO FURTHER—we transform learners into confident researchers by pairing hands-on methodology with real-world applications in qualitative analysis, systematic reviews, and advanced data science (NVivo, Python, R, GIS). Mentored directly by scholars trained at world-class institutions like the University of Waterloo, University of Toronto, and Brock University, our participants do not just build theoretical knowledge—they author publishable manuscripts and forge competitive portfolios for global graduate admissions and scholarships, turning shared curiosity into a brighter, global future.

Our Vision

Empowering the next generation of researchers with practical, hands-on methodology. Same curiosity, a brighter tomorrow. We bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world publication and admission successes.
