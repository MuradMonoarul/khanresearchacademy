Who We Are

About the Academy

Welcome to the Dr. Khan Research Academy. Building upon the foundational work of the Dr. Khan Social Impact Lab, the Academy serves as a dedicated hub for learning, mentorship, and capacity-building. We are committed to cultivating the next generation of reflexive, empowered scholars and practitioners who are equipped to tackle complex social challenges.

Grounded in a Freirean critical pedagogy, our Academy views education as a dialogic, collaborative, and transformative process. We move beyond traditional, top-down models of learning to create an environment where knowledge is co-produced and learners are active agents in their own development. Our programs are designed to bridge the gap between rigorous academic inquiry and real-world social impact, focusing on themes central to our lab's work:

The Future of Work Occupational Health Adult Learning Digital Transformations Social Equity

The Academy offers a vibrant intellectual home for students, early-career researchers, and professionals. Through a range of training programs, workshops, and mentorship opportunities, we foster critical thinking, methodological rigor, and a deep commitment to challenging structural inequalities. We believe that by nurturing a community of critical and compassionate thinkers, we can collectively imagine and build more just and inclusive futures.

✦ Future Outlook

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence for transformative education and research training, empowering a new generation of scholars and practitioners to create meaningful and equitable social change in Bangladesh and beyond.

✦ Core Commitments

Our Mission

  • ✓ To provide high-quality, critical, and participatory training in qualitative, mixed-methods, and participatory research methodologies.
  • ✓ To foster a collaborative and emancipatory learning environment where students, scholars, and practitioners can engage in dialogue and co-create knowledge.
  • ✓ To mentor and support the next generation of researchers and change-makers through structured programs, hands-on experience, and critical pedagogical approaches.
  • ✓ To translate rigorous research into accessible learning opportunities that inform policy, strengthen public understanding, and promote the well-being of workers and communities.
  • ✓ To uphold ethical, inclusive, and reflexive practices in all our educational programs, ensuring that we respect and amplify diverse voices and perspectives.
